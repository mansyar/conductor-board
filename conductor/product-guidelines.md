# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice

Conductor Board is a calm, precise developer tool. Its voice is utilitarian and
understated — no marketing, no filler, no hype. Every word should earn its place.

## Tone

- Neutral, helpful, direct.
- Prefer concrete verbs and short sentences.
- Assume the reader is a competent developer who wants the state, not an explanation
  of the obvious.

## UX Principles

1. **At-a-glance first.** The kanban must be readable in seconds; phase is conveyed
   by column position plus a consistent color accent.
2. **Minimal chrome.** No hero sections, animations for their own sake, or decorative
   elements. Space and typography do the work.
3. **Consistent phase semantics.** The four lifecycle colors are fixed and never reused
   for other meanings across the UI.
4. **Fast, direct actions.** Spec/plan view, copy path, and open-in-Zed are reachable
   in one or two interactions.
5. **Dark-theme friendly.** Default to a restrained dark palette with sufficient contrast.
6. **Sentence case labels.** Buttons, columns, and cards use sentence case.
